01

Three rules that decide everything

Every call in this plan comes from one of these. If you only remember three things, remember these three.

RULE 1

The clock belongs to one alliance

Occupation time is tracked per alliance, not per kingdom. Three alliances holding 70, 60 and 50 minutes all lose to one alliance holding 75.

So one alliance sits in the castle and keeps the clock. Everyone else feeds it.

RULE 2

Structures are cheap, cities are not

Fighting at the castle and turrets sends your casualties to the infirmary. Nothing dies until the infirmary is full.

Attacking somebody's city kills 35% of your own casualties permanently. That is why we want them attacking us, not the other way round.

RULE 3

You get up to 90% back

Field Triage returns 30% on its own and up to about 90% with satchels, rescue orders and alliance help.

So do not hoard troops. Troops sitting at home win nothing. The only real loss is infirmary overflow.

The one thing that actually costs you

Infirmary overflow. Once it is full, 30% of everything further is dead for good and the rest goes to the Enlistment Office for days. Heal in small batches all battle long. Set the heal slider to roughly 30 minutes, hit heal, spam alliance help, repeat. Save speedups for the moment you are actually about to overflow.

03

The five hours, in order

Times are shown against castle open. Set your battle time once and the page will mark the live phase and convert everything into your own clock.

Stored on your device only. Enter it in your own time zone and every phase below will show your local time.
T-3:00

Invasion phase

  • Attending castle battle? Stay out of fights. Empty your infirmary and keep it empty.
  • Not attending? This is your window. Go invade and do damage.
  • Snipe unreinforced enemies who look like joiner material, then move immediately. Do not sit still after a hit.
  • Do not chase a target into a cluster. That is how you feed them a free kill.
T-1:00

Teleport window, take the ground

  • Move in every alt, farm and non fighting account we have, the moment safe teleport opens.
  • The job is taking tiles, not blocking marches. A city does not slow an enemy march. What it does is occupy the tile, so if we own the close ground they have to land further out and march longer.
  • Priority tiles: the ring around the castle and the four turrets, and the ground around our leads. Take the tiles between the relic and the castle first, so their remaining approach runs through the slow zone and ours does not.
  • Rally leads teleport in last and tight, so march times to the castle stay short and even.
  • Do not teleport in early. Going in before the window opens gets you randomly relocated and strips your shield.
T+0:00

Take all four turrets

  • Turrets first, castle second. This is not optional and it is not the exciting job.
  • Four turrets held gives our castle squads the full lethality bonus and stops turret fire hitting us.
  • Assigned holders garrison and stay. Turret value builds the longer you hold it.
  • Fake rallies start now, but alts only. See the note below, because this tactic cuts the wrong way if real players join it.
  • Run the two tests in section 11 in the first ten minutes, on a turret, before we go anywhere near the castle.
Fake rallies pin us harder than them

A rally locks its own participants out of sending any other march until it resolves. Nothing stops a defender sitting in the castle from pulling their march out whenever they like. So a fake rally does not trap their garrison. It mostly discourages them from thinning it, while genuinely freezing whoever of ours joined.

So: fakes are launched by throwaway and alt accounts with at most one or two alt joiners. Anchors, skill carriers and turret retake cells never touch a fake rally. Make roughly one fake in three look heavy, or they will start reading march sizes and ignoring the alarm.

T+0:30

Let the turrets do the work

  • Their garrison is now taking fire from every turret we hold, and the fire is speeding up.
  • Bait bases push forward. Reinforce them properly, park them in front of enemy forces, and taunt.
  • Every attack they throw at a bait base costs them 35% of their losses permanently. Ours go to the infirmary.
  • Call out anyone who looks unreinforced or already wounded.
T+1:30

Take the seat and start the clock

  • Double rally to move their whale. First rally drains, second rally takes it, third seats the garrison.
  • Rallies must land within 5 seconds of each other. Aim for 1 to 2.
  • A WPK rally lands the killing blow. KVK softens. See the handoff warning below, it is the difference between banking our time and wasting it.
  • Maven seats the castle garrison the moment it is ours, with the backup anchor going in behind.
  • Hard deadline: the real assault goes in no later than T+1:45. No exceptions, even if the turret grind is going beautifully.
Why T+1:45 is a hard deadline

If they are sitting in the castle they are building toward a 2:30 unbroken hold, which ends the event instantly. If they took it at open, we have to break them before T+2:29 or we simply lose. And on total time, 2:30 is also roughly the break even point, so every minute past T+2:00 that they hold eats our fallback win too.

Letting them cook under four turrets is our best softening tool. Just never let it drift past the deadline, and keep one wave in reserve in case the first fails.

T+4:00

Protect the lead

  • Stop gambling. No speculative rallies that could cost us the seat.
  • Keep the turrets. Losing one late resets its fire rate and gives them a window.
  • If we are behind on total time, this is when everything goes in. If we are ahead, sit on it.
  • Freshest garrisons and emptiest infirmaries go in for the last 45 minutes. That is when their all in arrives.
Clock breaker rally, standing order

The moment their unbroken hold passes about 1:45, a reserve rally goes in whether or not it looks winnable. One successful flip, even held for seconds, wipes their consecutive progress back to zero.

The clock breaker is staffed by players who are not garrisoned and not in a retake cell, so it never cannibalises the seat.

T+5:00

Field Triage

  • Revive, then ask for help, then revive again. Help requests are free percentage.
  • Spend satchels and rescue orders. There is no reason to bank them.

In KvK this is kingdom wide

Turret control counts at kingdom level, not alliance level. Any of our alliances holding a turret still buffs whoever of ours is in the castle, and still stops that turret firing on them. So turret holders do not need to be in the clock alliance. Use everyone.

Four retake cells, one per turret

Once we hold the castle, flipping one of our turrets is the cheapest thing they can do to us. Assume they work that out and make it their main effort.

  • Retake inside 4 minutesA freshly flipped turret starts on the slow cadence, so a fast retake costs our seat about one volley. Every minute after that gets worse as it speeds up.
  • One cell per turret, staged next to itIndependent cells, because flipping two turrets at once is their best play and serialised responses will not cover it.
  • Cells are ring fencedRetake members are never in fake rallies, never skill carriers, never garrisoned somewhere else. They sit there looking bored on purpose.
  • Route around the relicTime a test march past it early so we know the real slow radius before it costs us a retake.
  • Staff them properlyNot whales, not pure alts. An alt only turret folds to any real rally and hands them a free reset lever.

The handoff is the biggest hole in this plan

If taking the castle gives occupancy to the winning rally's alliance, then a KVK rally that clears the seat puts KVK on the clock, and the swap over to WPK opens a vacancy for them to snipe. Two alliances each banking half the time is exactly how you dominate a battle and still lose it.

Preferred: KVK softens, a WPK rally lands last, so the clock starts on WPK. If KVK has to finish it: rehearse the handoff on a turret in the first hour. Count the roles before battle day, not during

Five structures to hold, and each one wants an anchor plus four skill carriers plus a spare carrier. That is about 30 named people, and then four retake cells, the rally squads and the clock breaker on top.

None of those roles can double up, because you only get one structure each and a rally locks you out of everything else. If we have not filled every slot by name in advance, we will be improvising the most important part of the battle live.

Nobody switches alliance mid march

Do not change alliance while you have a march garrisoned or sitting in a rally. We do not know what that does to the march or to the occupation clock, and the castle window is the wrong place to find out.

Anyone who switches into WPK reinforces turrets or joins rallies. They do not enter the castle without a leader clearing it first, for the reason in the next section.

06

Garrison marches

Holding is a different skill from hitting. Read this if you are seated in the castle or on a turret, or if you reinforce one.

One player carries the whole garrison's defence

In a garrison fight the game picks the single player with the highest stat bonuses as the source of defence bonuses for everyone in that structure. Four other members contribute one hero skill each on top.

So the seat is only as strong as its best player. If that player leaves to heal, the whole garrison quietly drops to the next best. Never let the anchor walk out without a replacement already inside.

The castle is a closed list, and this is why

Because the game picks the highest stat player, a strong attack built whale wandering into the castle can silently take the anchor role from Maven and replace good defensive bonuses with bad ones. Everyone in the seat gets worse and nothing on screen tells you it happened.

So only named players enter the castle. If you are not on the list, reinforce a turret or join a rally instead. This is the most likely way we beat ourselves today.

Rotating the anchor

Overlap, always. The replacement lands and confirms in chat before the tired march leaves. A1 pulls out, batch heals, comes back, and A2 carries the garrison in the meantime.

Never rotate the anchor and a skill carrier in the same five minutes. Degrade on one axis at a time.

They will siege the anchor's city instead of the castle

The smartest thing they can do is never rally our castle at all, and instead hammer Maven's city to fill Maven's infirmary with home defence wounded. Once that infirmary is full, our castle casualties start dying for real.

So anchors either keep a shield up all window, or strip their city entirely so a hit wounds almost nothing. Which of those applies depends on the shield test in section 11. Run it early.

Never zero a troop type

Top ups must be single type, or we lose our archers

Know your own two numbers

Before the window: your infirmary capacity, and how many troops you have committed between your garrison march and anything defending at home. If committed is bigger than your infirmary can hold, the excess becomes permanent deaths in a bad hour. Size your garrison march to fit.

Personal tripwire at 70% infirmary. Rotate out, batch heal, rotate back. Do not wait to be told.

07

Joiner presets, and why not everyone sends Chenko

This is the cheapest free damage available to us and most alliances get it wrong. It costs nothing to fix and it needs to be fixed before the window opens.

HOW IT WORKS

Only slot 1, only skill 1

When you join, the game reads the first hero in your lineup, and only that hero's first skill. Nothing else travels. Your gear does not matter.

A rally or garrison takes four joiner skills, and only from members whose first skill is maxed. If yours is not maxed you get skipped.

WHY IT MATTERS

Same hero stacks worse

Identical heroes add together. Different heroes multiply. Four of the same gives about 2.0x. Two plus two of different types gives about 2.25x.

That is roughly 12% more damage for free, purely from not all sending the same hero.

Target stacks

Garrison: Saul, Hilde, Chenko, Chenko. Tested in Season 3 against Amadeus / Petra / Marlin attackers and it won clearly, where Saul, Gordon, Gordon, Chenko lost the same fight.

Attack rally: mix Chenko and Amane rather than four Chenkos, and let the rally lead call it if they have scouted the garrison.

A maxed wrong hero is worse than an unmaxed one

An unmaxed hero gets skipped, which is harmless. But a maxed hero that arrives early holds that slot for the whole fight. A third Chenko turning up first does not add a new multiplier, it just pads one that is already there, and it locks out the Hilde that would have opened a fresh one.

So entry order matters: anchor goes in first, then carriers one at a time, each saying "in" in chat before the next launches. Once four are seated a leader calls slots locked, and only then does filler reinforcement go in.

Carrier rules

Carriers do not need gear, so mid sized accounts with the right maxed skill are perfect for it. Do not waste whales here, they are anchors and rally leads.

Carriers never join rallies, because the rally lock takes them out of circulation, and never hold a structure of their own, because you only get one. Keep one spare carrier inside each garrison, and stagger carrier swaps at least five minutes apart.

R4 and R5, before battle day

Post the list: player, which garrison, which slot 1 hero, and a fallback hero. Collect a screenshot from each carrier proving the skill is maxed. Verify it, do not take their word, because a wrong slot is invisible once the fight starts.

08

Who to hit, in order

Kills are not the win condition, so every hit on a person needs a reason. The reason is always "this removes a specific player from the castle fight".

Their garrison anchor

The one player supplying defence bonuses for their whole seat. Wound them, force them home, push them into overflow. The entire garrison drops when they leave. Nothing else you can do is worth this much.

Their castle rally leads

A rally uses nine skills from whoever starts it. Leads are scarce, joiners are not.

Their refill pipeline

Whoever is parked next to the castle topping the garrison back up. Cut the supply and the seat starves.

Anyone already wounded

A near full infirmary means your next hit turns their wounded into permanent dead.

Do not farm their joiners

Only four joiner slots count in any rally or garrison and they are interchangeable. Kill four and the next four fill in identically. You cannot drain a joiner bench, and you pay the 35% death rate to try.

Snipe joiner material during the invasion phase when it is cheap and undefended. During the castle window, go for the anchor and the leads.

Kill points only count near your own level

You only score against players whose Town Center is within 3 levels of yours. Check before you commit, or you will spend permanent troops for nothing.

09

Bodies, bait and noise

If you are an alt, a farm, or you are not strong enough to lead anything, this is your job and it genuinely changes the battle.

Do

  • Teleport in as soon as the window opens and park where you are told.
  • Surround our rally leads and joiners so enemy marches take longer to reach them.
  • Be a bait base. Take reinforcements, sit in front of their forces, and let them hit you.
  • Taunt. Emoji spam works. Angry players make worse decisions and attack bases they should leave alone.
  • Call out anything you see. Unshielded players, wounded players, marches forming.
  • Keep a random teleport ready and use it to escape.

Do not

  • Do not solo attack the castle or a turret. You get none of your attack bonuses and they get all of their defence bonuses.
  • Do not sit still after you hit somebody. Move.
  • Do not drop shield unless we have power near you.
  • Do not let your infirmary overflow while you are being farmed. Heal or move.
  • Do not go quiet. A spotted opportunity nobody hears about is worth nothing.
Why bait works here

When they attack one of our bases they permanently lose 35% of their casualties. Every swing they take at a properly reinforced bait base is a trade in our favour, and we get up to 90% back afterwards.

Expect it to convert against their careless players, not their good ones. Anyone competent scouts, sees the reinforcement and walks away. That is fine. It still costs them tempo and attention.

Bait duty and garrison duty are different people

Defending a bait base fills your own infirmary. If you do bait work and then get called into the castle, you arrive with a loaded hospital and become the weak link in the seat. Pick one job for the window.

Also, honestly: the 35% figure is confirmed for the attacker. We have not confirmed what the defender loses. Treat "defending is free" as a working assumption until we see the first real report, not a promise.

10

Talking to each other

Coordination beats power in this event. A mid strength alliance that executes cleanly out holds a stronger one that shows up disorganised.

VOICE

Be in voice

Rally timing in text does not work. Landing inside 5 seconds needs someone counting out loud.

CALLS

Say it in this order

Target, who leads, timer length, which hero joiners bring, exact launch time. Say it before launch, not as a correction afterwards.

ROUTES

Check the relic

Marches passing the relic slow down badly. Two leads who launch on the same second still land apart if one routed through it. Stand together before launching.

11

Check these in game before we commit

Honest list of what this plan assumes but nobody has confirmed. Run the first three on a turret in the opening ten minutes, before we commit anything to the castle. Guides get this stuff wrong, including the ones this plan was built from.

  • 1. Can two players garrison the same structureSend two different players' marches into our first captured turret. If the second one bounces, the whole multi player garrison plan collapses to one anchor plus rotation, and the joiner slot work only applies where stacking is actually allowed. Highest impact test we have.
  • 2. Who gets occupancy after a winning rallyFlip a turret with a KVK rally and watch whether KVK or the individual holds it. This is what decides whether WPK has to land the killing blow at the castle. Rehearse the handoff here where it is cheap.
  • 3. Does a shield survive while you are garrisonedDetermines whether anchors stay bubbled all window or strip their cities instead. Test on a turret holder.
  • 4. Real turret cadenceThe turret panel shows a live countdown to the next shot. Use the panel, not our 4 minute to 1 minute estimate, and have somebody calling it out during the fight.
  • 5. The relic slow radiusTime a march past it so we know how wide the slow zone actually is before it costs us a retake or desyncs a double rally.
  • 6. Window lengthTap the King's Castle on the world map and confirm the timer. Our plan assumes a 5 hour window with a 2:30 instant win. That has changed between updates before.
  • 7. What a bait defender actually losesRead the first real bait base report before we promise anyone that defending is free.
