T01The plan

The plan, in short

If you only read one part of this page, read this bit. Everything after it is detail.

  1. Take all four turrets as soon as the window opens, and hold them. They shoot at whoever is sitting in the castle, so we want them before we want the castle.
  2. Leave them in the castle for a while and let the turrets wear them down. The longer we hold a turret the faster it fires.
  3. Double rally them out, a WPK rally lands it and Maven garrisons. Then we hold it for 2:30 without a break, which ends the event and wins it outright at about T+2:55. No need to be there the full five hours.
  4. Everyone not holding something plays bait, so they waste troops attacking our bases. That costs them about 35% of their losses permanently, ours just go to the infirmary.
  5. Do not hold troops back. Everyone gets up to 90% of them back after the battle.

Why we are doing it that way

1

The clock is per alliance

If three of our alliances hold it for 70, 60 and 50 minutes, we still lose to one alliance that held it for 75. So WPK will be the one sitting in the castle, and everyone else supports that.

2

Attacking bases costs more than attacking the castle

Losses at the castle and turrets go to your infirmary. Attacking someone's base kills about 35% of your own casualties permanently, which is why we would rather they came at us.

3

You get up to 90% back

Field Triage gives most of it back after the battle, so there is no need to hold troops back.

Keep an eye on your infirmary

Once it is full, roughly 30% of anything after that dies permanently. Heal in small batches the whole way through. Set the slider to around 30 minutes, hit heal, ask for alliance help, and repeat. Save your speedups for when you are actually close to filling up.

T03Timeline

How the five hours go

Set the castle open time once and every phase switches to your own clock.

Saved on your device only.
T-3:00

Invasion phase

  • If you are staying for the castle, stay out of fights.
  • Snipe anyone unreinforced, then move straight away so you do not get caught.
T-2:00

Rebellion in the red zone

  • Do not be sitting in the alert zone at this point. Anyone already in there gets moved to a random spot on the map and loses their shield doing it. Stage outside and wait.
T-1:00

Teleport in and take ground

  • Move every alt and farm in. Take the tiles near the castle and turrets so they have to land further out.
  • Rally leads go in last and close together, so march times stay short and even.
  • Keep those alts and farms shielded and use them to reinforce our joiners and rally leads. A shielded base can still send reinforcements out, so they sit there as a troop reserve right where we need it.
  • The point is to throw their planning off early and make them work out where anything actually is.
T+0:00

Take all four turrets

  • Turrets first, and fast. They shoot whoever is in the castle, so we want them before we sit in it.
  • Whoever is assigned garrisons and stays put.
  • Start fake rallies, but alts only. See T06 for why.
T+0:20

Take the castle straight away

  • We are not waiting around for this. The earlier we are in, the earlier the 2:30 runs out and we can all go and do something else.
  • Double rally them. The first one drains, the second takes it, the third puts the garrison in.
  • They need to land within 5 seconds of each other, ideally 1 or 2.
  • A WPK rally takes it and Maven garrisons, so the clock starts on WPK.
T+0:25

Hold it for 2:30 straight

  • This is the whole thing now. Two and a half hours unbroken and the event ends with us winning, no matter how much time is left on the clock.
  • Keep all four turrets. If one flips, take it back quickly, because it starts shooting our own garrison.
  • Bait bases move out with reinforcements, then spam emojis at them to wind them up. Every time they attack one it costs them 35%.
  • Heal in batches the whole way through and keep the garrison topped up.
  • If they break our hold the timer goes back to zero, so take it straight back and start again.
T+2:55

This is where we win

  • 2:30 unbroken from when we took it, so give it an extra minute or two to be safe and that is the event over.
  • If our hold got broken at some point, we are into total time instead. Keep taking it back, most minutes overall wins at the five hour mark.
T+5:00

Field Triage

  • Revive, ask for help, then revive again. Use your satchels, there is no reason to save them.
T04Turrets

Turrets shoot whoever holds the castle

They are not there to protect whoever is inside, which is why we want them before we go for the castle.

Turrets heldWhat our castle squads get
1+8% lethalityBarely started.
2+12%They are starting to lose troops.
3+15%Good point to rally the castle.
4+20%They will not hold, go and take it.
Losing a turret resets it

A turret starts slow and speeds up to around a shot a minute, taking about 2% of the castle garrison each time. The cheapest thing they can do is send a throwaway march to flip one and reset its clock, so holding the turrets we already have matters more than grabbing extra ones. Keep four people parked next to their turret ready to take it back.

T06Changed

Changes from the first draft

A few things in the original plan turned out to work differently than we thought.

Was: fake rallies pin their joiners

Fake rallies should be alts only

A rally locks the people who joined it, and the defender can pull their garrison march out any time they want. So a fake rally ends up tying up our own people more than theirs. Use throwaway accounts for these.

Was: alts block enemy marches

Alts take up tiles, they do not slow marches down

Bases do not slow enemy marches down, only the relic does that. They do take up tiles though, so filling the ground near the castle still forces them to land further away.

Was: KVK clears, Maven moves in

WPK should be the one that takes the castle

If the castle goes to whichever alliance won the rally, then a KVK rally taking it puts the time on KVK instead of WPK and splits our total in half. So KVK softens them up and WPK finishes it. Worth testing on a turret early on to see how it actually behaves.
